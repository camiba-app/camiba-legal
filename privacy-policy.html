Privacy Policy

Camiba · Effective 20 October 2026

Camiba is a personal snack tasting journal for iPhone and iPad. The journal stays in the app sandbox on your device. Camiba does not run a server for your snacks, photos, taste pages, or credit balance.

Account on this device

You open Camiba with one tap. There is no password and no Sign in with Apple. The app stores a local session on the device. Log Out clears that session only. Your snacks, photos, taste pages, and Snack Credits remain, and you tap Continue again to open them. Delete Account removes the local account and the snacks, photos, taste pages, and unspent credits written for that account. The next Continue starts a new local account. Neither action uploads a deletion request to a Camiba server, because the account exists only on this device.

Information you add

When you add a snack, Camiba stores the name, brand, category, taste notes, price, store, rating, verdict, and any photo you attach. A finished taste page keeps a copy of those fields, so the page stays if you later delete the snack. Sample snacks that appear in an empty journal are stored on the device. They are not a live feed from a network.

Camera, photos, microphone, and speech

Camiba asks for a permission only after you tap the matching control. Take Photo uses the camera for a still picture of packaging or the snack. Choose Photo reads the photo library so you can import a picture. Save Image writes a taste page into your photo library. Speak Notes uses the microphone and on-device speech recognition for the field you choose, and the text is saved only after you confirm it. Camiba does not request these permissions at launch. Camiba does not ask for tracking permission or for push notifications.

Purchases

Snack Credit packs are consumable in-app purchases. Apple charges the price shown on the pack. Camiba records a verified transaction identifier and grants the matching credits once. The same transaction is not applied again. Cancelling the Apple sheet does not change your balance. A new account may receive a starter balance that is stored on the device and is not a purchase. Camiba does not offer a restore-purchases control for these consumable credits. If Apple refunds a pack, Camiba removes only the unused remainder of that grant. Finished taste pages stay on the device.
