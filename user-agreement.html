User Agreement

Camiba · Effective 20 October 2026

Camiba lets you keep a personal record of snacks and compile that record into a taste page stored on your device. By tapping Continue you agree to this User Agreement and the Privacy Policy.

Opening the app

Continue is the only sign-in. Camiba does not offer a password, an email login, or Sign in with Apple. Log Out ends the session on this device and leaves your journal in place. Delete Account removes the local account and the journal data written for it, including any advertising identifier and notification token stored on this device. You confirm Delete Account in the app before anything is removed.

Tracking and notifications

After the journal opens, Apple may ask whether Camiba may track which of Collection, Add Entry, Taste Pages, and Settings you open, and whether it may send notifications. Snack photos are not sold for advertising. You can refuse either request and keep using the journal.

Your journal

You can add, edit, and delete snack entries without spending Snack Credits. A taste page costs 1 Snack Credit and is charged only after the page is saved. Previewing a page does not spend a credit. If saving fails or you cancel, no credit is spent. Trying the same page again does not charge a second credit for a page that already saved. Deleting a finished page does not return the credit. Deleting a snack does not delete a page that already includes it. The page marks that snack as deleted.

Snack Credits

Snack Credits are consumable. A starter balance may be placed on a new local account and is not an Apple purchase. Additional packs are sold by Apple at the prices shown in the app. Credits have no cash value. Camiba does not restore consumable packs from a button in the app. A refund from Apple removes only the unused remainder of that pack. Finished taste pages remain.

Clearing data

Clear All Data, after you confirm it, permanently deletes snacks, photos, taste pages, and unspent credits on this device. Log Out does not do that. Delete Account does remove that local account and its journal.
